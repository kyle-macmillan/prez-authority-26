# Preliminary findings: path-dependent presidential directives

## Target quantity

The target is the proportion of eligible presidential directives for which non-vesting
evidence supports path-dependent drafting.  Membership in the interview-supported HC union
is a positive-control designation, not itself proof of path dependency.

## What can currently be reported

- Full nonceremonial corpus: 13,461 directives.
- Scoped corpus after narrow personal/diplomatic exclusions: 13,086.
- Children with resolved direct-transition links: 2,382.
- Observed-link lower bound: 17.7% of the full corpus and 18.2% of the scoped corpus.
- No-explicit-link inference universe: 10,704 directives.
- Corrected proposed positive-control union: 1,577 directives; this remains a control-pool
  count, not a path-dependency prevalence estimate.

## Metric evidence

On 200 known-parent children with directive identifiers masked, 5-word distinctive reuse
performs best: 50.0% recall at rank 1, 70.0% at rank 5, and 75.0% at rank 10.  This supports
candidate-set retrieval, not a high-confidence single-parent classification.

The predeclared development rule required at least 90% known-parent precision among at
least 20 selected cases.  No combination of reuse score, first/second margin, and
cross-method consensus met that standard.  The best qualifying-size development subset was
21/24 (87.5%).  The untouched holdout was not used to rescue or retune the failed rule.

## Interpretation

The study can presently give a defensible lower bound based on observed links.  It cannot
yet give a defensible high-confidence point estimate for implicit path dependency.  The
failure is informative: deterministic text methods retrieve useful top-five candidate sets,
but their rank-1 certainty is insufficient for the proposed 90% claim under the reduced
human-validation design.

## Exploratory work kept separate

The top-five candidate file preserves 5-word reuse, the original 10-word/semantic hybrid,
and a 5-word/semantic hybrid for 486 pilot children.  These can support qualitative family
discovery and future review, but they are not included in the high-confidence numerator.
