# Memos 
- Many include paragraph "you are authorized and directed to transmit...," or "...directed to
publish the memo...". Decided to remove these.
- On identifying legally binding documents: sometimes have disclaimer (see e.g.
https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/memorandum-the-freedom-information-act). These would be
broken up into a new paragraph according to the rules I've created, but affect whole document. 
- Also have disclaimer saying this is internal management (see e.g. https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/memorandum-community-right-know-initiatives)
- Do we include the "justification for the President's determination" (see e.g. https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/memorandum-determination-and-waiver-romanias-ineligibility-under-section-129-the-atomic)
- What is the proper cutoff length for list items? Some are very long, others shorter. 
  - Useful for deciding when to merge as well. (see e.g. https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/letter-congressional-leaders-reporting-the-executive-order-blocking-property-certain-0)
- When there are sections present, don't label anything as boilerplate.
