How the source corpus becomes the reported n

Source document IDs run as high as 20,232, but IDs are not contiguous. This analysis starts from the 16,397-document development corpus—the main analysis split used to construct and rank parent candidates. The separately reserved holdout split is not used in this ranking. The codebook-based ceremonial filter excludes 5,437 directives, leaving 10,960 directives eligible to appear as children or parents. Automatic reference matching identifies parents for 2,062 children, leaving 8,898 unresolved children for candidate ranking. Candidate 1 exists for 8,894 children and Candidate 2 for 8,890. Those are candidate-availability counts, not the chart n.

All three plotted channels are segment-level measures. A pair receives an operative embedding, trigram TF-IDF, or text-reuse score only when both its child and parent have at least one extracted operative segment. Therefore, the charts omit candidate pairs for which the child, parent, or both have no operative segment:

A directive has no extracted operative segment when, after authority masking and boilerplate removal, no passage matches the rule-based patterns for a directed operative action. Common cases are letters that transmit or report information, narrative or explanatory documents, and actionable language phrased outside the recognized ordering forms. This is a measurement limitation: it does not mean that the directive is legally unimportant or has no effect. In this analysis, 2,490 of the 10,960 eligible directives have zero extracted operative segments (executive order: 36, letter: 2,001, memorandum: 431, proclamation: 22).

How to read these metrics

  • Operative embedding similarity: semantic similarity between the strongest aligned operative segments.
  • Word-trigram TF-IDF similarity: overlap in distinctive case-sensitive three-word sequences.
  • Text reuse: average reused-word count among the strongest aligned segment pairs.
  • Why Candidate 2 can have a smaller n: the earliest directives of each type may have fewer than two eligible earlier parents, and directives without operative segments have missing channel scores.
