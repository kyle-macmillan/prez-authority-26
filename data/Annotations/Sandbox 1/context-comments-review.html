L18

George Bush · June 6, 1989 · letter to Congress
Jennifer Nou: “Ordering language requires context from preamble”

Preamble excluded from directive

This report is being transmitted pursuant to Section 7(a)(2) of the Export-Import Bank Act of 1945, as amended (12 U.S.C. 635e(a)(2)).

Extracted order action

Based on the information supplied by the Export-Import Bank, I have determined that for fiscal year 1989 (i) the amount of direct loan authority available to the Bank is sufficient and (ii) the amount of guarantee authority available to the Bank may be greater than needed. Although there could be substantial excess guarantee authority, I believe that its continued availability will be advantageous. Guarantees represent an important means of support for U.S. exports and involve only a limited subsidy. Moreover, a portion of any unused 1989 authority will be carried over into 1990 when demand may be higher. Therefore, I do not seek legislation to rescind any authority of the Bank. I have concluded that the statutory fiscal year 1989 limits for the Export-Import Bank authority should remain unchanged.

Why the context matters: the extracted text looks like a substantive policy determination in isolation. The preceding sentence identifies the document’s function as a statutorily required report to Congress, supporting Jennifer’s document-level classification of “Operative Actions to Congress.”
