Vesting Authority Specificity Analysis

This report analyzes 18,418 presidential directives in the development and holdout corpora. Each presidential term is reported as a separate administration.

When categories overlap. Categories are not mutually exclusive across the constitutional and statutory families: a directive may receive one specific constitutional category—(2) or (3)—and one specific statutory category—(5) or (6)—when its vesting clause cites both kinds of authority. Within each family, the categories are hierarchical and mutually exclusive: (3) supersedes (2), and (6) supersedes (5). Categories (1), (4), and (7) apply only when no specific authority is detected, so they do not overlap with the specific-authority categories. Categories (8) and (9) are mutually exclusive coverage categories for, respectively, directives with no extracted vesting clause and directives with an extracted but unclassified vesting clause. A directive is counted at most once in each applicable category, so category totals should not be added together.

Category Definitions

CategoryRule
(1) Generic ConstitutionThe Constitution is the only authority cited.
(2) Specific constitutionA constitutional Article or the Chief Executive role is cited, without a more specific constitutional provision.
(3) Specific provision in ConstitutionA constitutional section, Amendment, named Clause, Commander in Chief role, or pardon/reprieve power is cited.
(4) Generic StatuteGeneric law or statute wording is the only authority cited.
(5) An Act of CongressA named or referenced Act, numbered Public Law, Act of Congress, or authorizing joint resolution is cited without a specific statutory section.
(6) Specific statutory sectionA statutory section or subsection, U.S.C. provision, title, or chapter is cited; this category supersedes An Act of Congress.
(7) Constitution and laws of the United StatesThe combined Constitution-and-laws boilerplate is the only authority cited.
(8) No vesting clauseNo vesting clause was extracted.
(9) Other/unclassified vesting authorityA vesting clause was extracted, but none of categories (1)-(7) applies.

Method

The analysis first applies the project's existing vesting-clause extractor. Within each extracted clause, it retains sources presented as presidential authority through wording such as by, under, pursuant to, including, or authority conferred/granted by. A proclamation's Whereas recital is retained only when it makes a first-person authority assertion or states that Congress or law authorizes, empowers, or directs the President to act. Contextual uses of pursuant to within other recitals are discarded.

Citations appearing only as compliance, purpose, or context are excluded, including citations following consistent with, in accordance with, in order to, in furtherance of, in light of, and as contemplated by. Matching tolerates capitalization, punctuation, singular/plural forms, and observed OCR variants; it does not use fuzzy matching.
