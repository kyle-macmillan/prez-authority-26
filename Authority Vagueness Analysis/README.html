Vesting Authority Specificity Analysis

This report analyzes 20,232 presidential directives in the development and holdout corpora. Each presidential term is reported as a separate administration.

Mutually exclusive categories. Every directive receives exactly one category. Constitutional and statutory specificity are classified independently and then combined. Constitutional Articles, roles, and provisions share one specific-Constitution level; named Acts and statutory provisions share one specific-statute level. The resulting eight substantive categories are accompanied by two bookkeeping buckets for no extracted vesting clause and other/unclassified authority. Additional citations to Executive Orders, treaties, agreements, or similar authorities do not displace a recognized constitutional/statutory combination. Category sums are checked against directive totals for every administration and directive-type view.

Category Definitions

CategoryRule
(1) Generic Constitution onlyThe Constitution is cited without statutory authority.
(2) Specific Constitution onlyA constitutional Article, section, Amendment, named Clause, Chief Executive or Commander in Chief role, or pardon/reprieve power is cited without statutory authority.
(3) Generic Statute onlyGeneric law or statute wording is cited without constitutional authority.
(4) Specific Statute onlyA named or referenced Act, numbered Public Law, Act of Congress, or authorizing joint resolution, statutory section or subsection, U.S.C. provision, title, or chapter is cited without constitutional authority.
(5) Generic Constitution + Generic StatuteGeneric constitutional and statutory authority are both cited.
(6) Generic Constitution + Specific StatuteGeneric constitutional authority and a specific Act or statutory provision are cited.
(7) Specific Constitution + Generic StatuteA specific constitutional source and generic statutory authority are cited.
(8) Specific Constitution + Specific StatuteA specific constitutional source and a specific Act or statutory provision are cited.
No vesting clauseNo vesting clause was extracted.
Other/unclassified vesting authorityA vesting clause was extracted, but no constitutional or statutory authority was classified.

Method

The analysis first applies the project's rule-based vesting-clause extractor. Strong signals include vested in me (plus the reviewed OCR form vested in my by), by virtue of [the/my] authority, the historical authority vested in the President formula, and narrowly reviewed pursuant to my authority constructions. Sentence-opening pursuant to or under language must contain a statutory or constitutional citation.

Conditional rules retain authority appearing before or after an ordering phrase, including It is hereby ordered, pursuant to…, I hereby order, by the authority vested in me…, and pursuant to [citation], I determine/I hereby exempt…. The last construction selects the nearest authority marker and does not cross intervening consistent with, in accordance with, or in response to language. Parenthetical citations are protected during sentence splitting, and internal citation commas are retained through U.S.C., Statutes at Large, Public Law, amendment, and additional-provision components.

Within each extracted clause, the analysis retains sources presented as presidential authority through wording such as by, under, pursuant to, including, or authority conferred/granted by. A proclamation's Whereas recital is retained only when it makes a first-person authority assertion or states that Congress or law authorizes, empowers, or directs the President to act. Contextual uses of pursuant to within other recitals are discarded.

Citations appearing only as compliance, purpose, or context are excluded, including citations following consistent with, in accordance with, in order to, in furtherance of, in light of, and as contemplated by. Matching tolerates capitalization, punctuation, singular/plural forms, and observed OCR variants; it does not use fuzzy matching.
