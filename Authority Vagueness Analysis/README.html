Category Definitions

CategoryRule
(1) Generic Constitution onlyThe Constitution is cited without statutory authority.
(2) Specific Constitution onlyA constitutional Article or the Chief Executive role is cited, without a more specific constitutional provision or statutory authority.
(3) Specific constitutional provision onlyA constitutional section, Amendment, named Clause, Commander in Chief role, or pardon/reprieve power is cited without statutory authority.
(4) Generic Statute onlyGeneric law or statute wording is cited without constitutional authority.
(5) Act of Congress onlyA named or referenced Act, numbered Public Law, Act of Congress, or authorizing joint resolution is cited without constitutional authority or a specific statutory section.
(6) Specific statutory section onlyA statutory section or subsection, U.S.C. provision, title, or chapter is cited without constitutional authority.
(7) Generic Constitution + Generic StatuteGeneric constitutional and statutory authority are both cited.
(8) Generic Constitution + Act of CongressGeneric constitutional authority and an Act of Congress are cited.
(9) Generic Constitution + Specific statutory sectionGeneric constitutional authority and a specific statutory section are cited.
(10) Specific Constitution + Generic StatuteA constitutional Article or the Chief Executive role and generic statutory authority are cited.
(11) Specific Constitution + Act of CongressA constitutional Article or the Chief Executive role and an Act of Congress are cited.
(12) Specific Constitution + Specific statutory sectionA constitutional Article or the Chief Executive role and a specific statutory section are cited.
(13) Specific constitutional provision + Generic StatuteA specific constitutional provision and generic statutory authority are cited.
(14) Specific constitutional provision + Act of CongressA specific constitutional provision and an Act of Congress are cited.
(15) Specific constitutional provision + Specific statutory sectionA specific constitutional provision and a specific statutory section are cited.
(16) No vesting clauseNo vesting clause was extracted.
(17) Other/unclassified vesting authorityA vesting clause was extracted, but no constitutional or statutory authority was classified.

Method

The analysis first applies the project's existing vesting-clause extractor. Within each extracted clause, it retains sources presented as presidential authority through wording such as by, under, pursuant to, including, or authority conferred/granted by. A proclamation's Whereas recital is retained only when it makes a first-person authority assertion or states that Congress or law authorizes, empowers, or directs the President to act. Contextual uses of pursuant to within other recitals are discarded.

Citations appearing only as compliance, purpose, or context are excluded, including citations following consistent with, in accordance with, in order to, in furtherance of, in light of, and as contemplated by. Matching tolerates capitalization, punctuation, singular/plural forms, and observed OCR variants; it does not use fuzzy matching.
